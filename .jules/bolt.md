## 2025-03-01 - Optimize MMR Diversity Loop By Maintaining Max Similarity Index
**Learning:** In MMR (Maximal Marginal Relevance) deduplication (`_mmr_dedup`), computing the diversity penalty involves checking a candidate's similarity against *all* previously selected chunks from the same document. A naive implementation does a nested loop over selected chunks resulting in O(K * N * S * D) complexity (where K is top_k, N is remaining chunks, S is selected chunks, and D is embedding dimension). This causes severe algorithmic slowdowns on long documents with many chunks.
**Action:** Replace the nested loop by maintaining a `max_sims` array for all N candidate chunks. When a new chunk is selected, immediately loop over the remaining chunks and update their `max_sims` by performing a single O(N * D) pass against the *newly* selected chunk. This drops the overall selection loop complexity down to strictly O(K * N * D) operations and guarantees O(1) reads for the max similarity during the selection phase.
